about

I'm a designer, photographer, and writer working out of Chicago. Most projects start with a question I can't stop turning over — what does a coffee shop's ten-year-old regular think when the cups change? What does a street look like at the one hour of day nobody photographs it? I like work that answers those questions plainly.

Previously: in-house designer at a transit startup, freelance photographer for local publications, and a very long stint writing show notes nobody read.
