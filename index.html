about

I'm a designer, photographer, and writer studying at the University of Connecticut. I plan on researching the connection between Artifical Intelligence, Design and Digital Ethics, Consumer Privacy and Data, and Internet Culture. I am working towards a Digital Media and Design major with a minor in Political Science on the Pre-Law track. I am also enrolled in a Fast-Track Undergraduate program to work towards gaining a Master's in Public Policy.

Currently: Marketing Assistant at UConn's Institutional Insights and Innovation, Digital Producer at UConn's Daily Campus, Communications & Digital Media Specialist at UConn's Honors and Enrichment Programs.
